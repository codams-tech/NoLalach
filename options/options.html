Incognito Loophole Warning

Checking…

Without Incognito access, a trader can bypass TraderLock by opening broker websites in an Incognito window. Fix this now:

  1. Open chrome://extensions in a new tab
  2. Find TraderLock in the list
  3. Click "Details"
  4. Toggle "Allow in Incognito" to ON
